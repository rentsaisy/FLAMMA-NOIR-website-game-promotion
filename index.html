01 / The story

Every mystery leaves
a trail.

Katerine, a newly appointed lawyer at NOIR, an investigative organization specializing in mysterious cases. Her first assignment involves investigating a mysterious forest arson that has destroyed the natural habitat of the Blushfen, a rare species of pink fox.

Alongside the NOIR investigation team, Katerine must gather evidence, uncover hidden secrets, and identify the mastermind behind the incident.
